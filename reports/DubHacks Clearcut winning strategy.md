# Ship one plot for Community Kitchen

Clearcut enters **Community Kitchen** as its only title track, plus three sponsored side tracks (**Perceptron perception, SpaceXAI, and SideShift**) and **DubHacks NEXT**. The app a judge can score in one visit is a local, offline evidence pack for one fictional community cooperative and one real plot in West Kalimantan (**−0.78, 110.08**): September 2019 Sentinel-2 canopy beside the August 2026 frame, Hansen loss in 2023, Perceptron Mk1.5 boxes already computed, and one sentence a community monitor is allowed to say. **Main EUDR duties apply on 30 December 2026 to large and medium operators; most micro and small operators wait until 30 June 2027**, and the law has already slipped twice. Track applicability is **30%** of the score. The reason on screen is social good: people who live with the forest get evidence they can read and show. The demo runs from files already on the laptop. SpaceXAI’s “real space data” line is a scoring risk even after a real Grok Voice call makes the team eligible. Treat **Sunday 10:00 AM PDT** as the Devpost lock.

## Community Kitchen earns the 30% when the user is the community

A team must submit to exactly one title track. The three DubHacks tracks share one hardware menu and one rubric; Visa is the cash title track and uses a different scorecard ([Title tracks](https://dubhacks.notion.site/26-Title-Tracks-bbaff32c1d7e8308be7d8185bd983487), [Devpost](https://dubhacks-2026.devpost.com/), [Prizes](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/7f2ff32c1d7e83cbb22981e0c329c033)). Organizers tell teams that fit is the most important choice, ahead of impact, automation, or novelty ([Picking a Track](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/3faff32c1d7e8089ac4ad35c334c1457)). The published rubric makes that instruction numeric: **Functionality 30%, Track Applicability 30%, Design 20%, Creativity 20%**, each on a 1–8 scale. Excellent applicability means the project is an exemplary demonstration of the track. Fair means a superficial connection ([Official Judges Rubric](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/154ff32c1d7e8304a76b011636f6732f)).

Community Kitchen’s prompt is technology that helps people thrive: accessibility, healthcare, civic tech, emergency response, and community support. The Hacker Guide adds sustainability and equity ([Title tracks](https://dubhacks.notion.site/26-Title-Tracks-bbaff32c1d7e8308be7d8185bd983487), [Hacker Guide](https://dubhacks.notion.site/The-DubHacks-26-Hacker-Guide-15dff32c1d7e83748aac8154036aa658)). The winning shape is social good. A fictional community in West Kalimantan already knows the clearing is happening. Satellite maps and buyer reports are written for someone else. Clearcut gives the people on that land a pack they can read without a GIS degree and show to a local authority: dated pictures, the loss year, what the land became, and a second region the model will not confirm. That is community support. The screen has to be readable by that person. Plain language, one plot, the inconclusive box left visible.

Open the pitch with that purpose. Say “social good” in the first sentence and in the Devpost overview. A procurement dashboard uses the same pixels and lands in the fair band. If a NEXT or SideShift judge asks who pays, answer then: a community forestry fund covers the pack so the village does not depend on the buyer’s own report. Leave that sentence out of the three-minute pitch.

Chef’s Choice loses because its prompt asks for technology that is imaginative, expressive, and fun: games, art, music, creative tools ([Title tracks](https://dubhacks.notion.site/26-Title-Tracks-bbaff32c1d7e8308be7d8185bd983487)). Devpost labels the matching prize “Chef's Kitchen”; Notion uses one shared title-track prize menu. Treat them as the same track. A clean evidence UI can score Design. It does not become an exemplary fun project. Visa: Reimagine Shopping pays **$5,000 split across the team** and is the only named-judge track on Devpost (Lisa Kapil). Its prompt is a generative commerce journey through discovery, comparison, personalization, checkout, payments, loyalty, or post-purchase, and Commerce Impact is 15% of a separate rubric whose UX weight is internally inconsistent (25% in the bullets, 20% in the table) ([VISA Judging Rubric](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/3efff32c1d7e809d9063de7d7157d957), [Hacker Guide](https://dubhacks.notion.site/The-DubHacks-26-Hacker-Guide-15dff32c1d7e83748aac8154036aa658)). A land-evidence screen does not become that journey by adding a buy button. Visa-only teams are judged on a 3-minute video with no ballroom visit; a Visa team that also enters a side track should plan on the ballroom as well ([Submissions and Judging](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/14eff32c1d7e837793e2018a30a234cb)). Prize value does not distinguish the three hardware tracks. No source publishes how many teams enter each one, so “less crowded” is a guess, not a reason. The reason is the rubric.

Functionality’s excellent band is that the project successfully works in the room, with robust execution. A precomputed path that a judge can click meets that bar. A screenshot deck falls toward fair or insufficient. Creativity’s insufficient band is a project that merely replicates existing approaches. Sentinel-2 beside Hansen is a known pattern. The creative claim is the constraint: one Perceptron box that Hansen supports, and a second box the model will not confirm ([Official Judges Rubric](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/154ff32c1d7e8304a76b011636f6732f)).

## The pitch is six beats and one legal sentence

The person on screen is a community monitor for a fictional cooperative that already holds a coordinate and has to show what happened to their forest. Upstream operators who first place a covered product on the EU market, or export it, must collect plot coordinates and may use remotely sensed images to check them. The Commission does not name a satellite product or a minimum resolution, and it says a global forest map is not a definitive compliance source ([Commission FAQ](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/publications/faq-eudr-implementation_en)). Downstream SME traders, after the December 2025 simplification, keep supplier and buyer records. They do not run this satellite case ([Green Forum, roles](https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/nature-and-biodiversity/implementing-eu-deforestation-regulation-eudr/roles-and-responsibilities_en)). Auditors review the file. They are not the hero of a three-minute demo.

The date line, in small type on the first card: **Articles 3 to 13 and the related duties apply from 30 December 2026.** Operators who are natural persons or micro or small undertakings, and who already had that status by 31 December 2024, generally wait until **30 June 2027**, except timber products already covered by the EU Timber Regulation, which stay on 30 December 2026. Medium operators are on the December date. The regulation slipped from 30 December 2024, then from 30 December 2025, via Regulation (EU) 2025/2650 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj/eng), [Commission topic page](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/forests/deforestation/regulation-deforestation-free-products_en)). On 4 May 2026 the Commission said it would not propose another change to the basic text ([COM(2026) 191](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A52026DC0191)). A third political delay is still discussed in trade press and is not a legislative act ([FoodNavigator, 5 October 2026](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/10/05/eudr-will-it-be-simplified-further/)). Say the date is law today. Do not say “large operators only.”

“Deforestation” in the regulation means conversion of forest to agricultural use. “Deforestation-free” means the commodity was not produced on land deforested after **31 December 2020**. A plantation is agricultural use. One photographed box does not identify a shipment, a legality check, or an authority’s decision ([consolidated Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A02023R1115-20251226)). The sentence the app is allowed to say, on the confirmed box only, is: **“This plot shows post-2020 tree-cover loss consistent with plantation conversion.”** Under it: not a compliance finding. The second box stays on screen as **inconclusive**. Hansen’s loss layer ends in 2024, so the August 2026 smoke is what the picture shows and is not a 2026 Hansen detection.

Judges will cut a pitch at three minutes inside one assigned 50-minute round in the HUB Ballroom. The 50 minutes is the time the team stays at the table, because side-track judges can arrive at any moment. It is not the length of one visit ([Submissions and Judging](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/14eff32c1d7e837793e2018a30a234cb)).

| Beat | What is on screen | What is said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “For social good.” Fictional community, coordinates −0.78, 110.08, cutoff 31 December 2020, date line 30 December 2026 / 30 June 2027 | “For the people on this land. One real plot. We will not call anyone non-compliant.” |
| 2 | Sentinel-2, full width: September 2019 canopy, August 2026 grid, roads, and smoke | “Both frames are real Sentinel-2.” |

| 6 | Second box, same layout, verdict “Inconclusive” | “If both boxes said plantation, the first result would be theater.” |

Then stop. If a judge asks whether the supplier is illegal, play the recorded answer: the app saw one plot’s change. It did not trace a shipment, check the law of the country of production, or decide negligible risk. Member State authorities do that. A second recorded line answers “what am I looking at?” Free-form voice, a Borneo map, a supplier list, NDVI, a risk score, a red “EUDR fail” badge, a mock due diligence statement, accounts, uploads, and any live tile or model call are cut. NDVI does not separate rainforest from a mature plantation, and the Commission does not certify a model ([Commission FAQ](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/publications/faq-eudr-implementation_en)). The honesty beat buyers already use is a loss pixel that imagery does not confirm ([GFW Pro](https://pro-news.globalforestwatch.org/news-events/3-ways-gfw-pro-can-help-commodity-processors-and-traders-go-deforestation-free)).

## Missing draft, missing video, or a private-only repo ends the weekend

The Hacker Guide and Devpost agree, and the marketing site does not. **Saturday 17 October, before 10:30 PM PDT**, the draft must include every teammate as a contributor with Discord handles, the tracks, and an overview of the project. No Saturday draft means no Sunday judging. **Sunday 18 October, before 10:00 AM PDT**, Devpost locks. The guide calls 10:00 AM a hard lock and says finals are due sharply then. Devpost’s banner is the same instant. The public schedule’s “10:30AM Submissions Due!” matches the judging workshop, not the lock ([Devpost](https://dubhacks-2026.devpost.com/), [Devpost dates](https://dubhacks-2026.devpost.com/details/dates), [Hacker Guide](https://dubhacks.notion.site/The-DubHacks-26-Hacker-Guide-15dff32c1d7e83748aac8154036aa658), [dh26.dubhacks.co](https://dh26.dubhacks.co/)). Submit as if 10:00 is the only clock. Use 10:30 only as the time to be in the ballroom for the workshop, arriving about 10:20.

The final submission must include a recorded presentation. **Teams who do not submit a video will not be allowed to judge.** No general runtime is specified. Visa’s 3-minute cut is for Visa. Code must be uploaded before the deadline and linked on Devpost. A working demo with no code uploaded is a disqualification. The recommended upload is a **public GitHub repository** ([Hacker Guide](https://dubhacks.notion.site/The-DubHacks-26-Hacker-Guide-15dff32c1d7e83748aac8154036aa658)). [github.com/jstnzhou/Clearcut](https://github.com/jstnzhou/Clearcut) is the repo. The 70/30 rule is a code-origin rule, not a library rule. At most **30%** of the code may be code not written during the hackathon, and at least **70%** must be written during it. Anything presented should have been worked on from **12:30 PM Saturday to 10:00 AM Sunday**. Ideation before that is allowed. Public frameworks and libraries are allowed. There is no published AI-coding ban, and the guide does not say how the percentage is measured ([Hacker Guide](https://dubhacks.notion.site/The-DubHacks-26-Hacker-Guide-15dff32c1d7e83748aac8154036aa658)). The practical reading for this repo: the screens, the local player, the voice fallback, and the Devpost narrative are written tonight and are the bulk of what gets committed. Pre-event pipeline scripts stay a minority of the repository. Shipping the old pipeline as most of the visible code collides with the rule. Leaving after the main-track judge, while those three are still walking, drops the side prizes ([Submissions and Judging](https://dubhacks.notion.site/p/14eff32c1d7e837793e2018a30a234cb)).

## Conclusion

The score this team can still move tonight is applicability plus a demo that works with the network unplugged. Community Kitchen, Perceptron, SideShift, and NEXT are one story told at different lengths: social good for the people on the land, a pack they can show someone, the model as a step in that pack, and a team that will keep building it. SpaceXAI is the odd slot. Eligibility is cheap and real, and the prompt’s space-data sentence can still mark the project down, which is a reason to spend twenty minutes on one Voice call and no time on a generated sky. The sentence on the screen is the whole product. Widening it into a compliance verdict, a supplier score, or a live map spends the hours the 10:00 AM lock will not give back.
